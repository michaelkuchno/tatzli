# Content review – stories & songs (engagement pass)

Goal: every story and song should make a 3-year-old (and siblings up to 7) *want* to watch and join in, while staying educational.
Approach: **edit, don't replace**. Sentences that already worked were left alone, because every changed sentence has to be re-recorded in 4 languages.

## Rubric (1–5)

| | Criterion |
|---|---|
| A | Hook & tension: a small problem, surprise or "where is…?" |
| B | Repetition / refrain to say along (2–4×) |
| C | Humour: gentle slapstick, a silly mistake |
| D | Participation: answer, point, make a sound or movement (TPR) |
| E | Concrete, picturable language; short sentences (L1: 3–6 words) |
| F | Warm, satisfying ending; no fear, violence or moralising |
| G | Songs: rhythm, rhyme, catchy repeated line, actions |

For songs, A is scored as "fun premise" and C as "playfulness" (funny sounds, silly bits). Bedtime songs get calm, gentle humour (a snoring teddy) rather than slapstick.

## Bernese forms I was less sure of (please check with berndeutsch.ch)

- «Tatzli **hebt sech d Ohre zue**» – *zuehebe* = to hold shut.
- «Tatzli **leit ne um e Haus – wie ne Schau**» – *Haus* = Hals and *Schau* = Schal, as in words.ts; *leit ne* = legt ihn.
- «Tatzli **schüttlet sech** wie ne Hund», «Tatzli **schleckt**».
- «**Dä** isch **fasch** so gross wie Tatzli».
- «**Wele Buechstabe hesch du?**» – *wele* follows the existing «Wele Stei».
- «d Nase **as Fänschter**», «**dür e Chäs**», «so **fescht**».
- «**Mach di** gross / chly», «**Los mau!**», «**No nid!**», «**suberi Häng**», «**streckt sech**», «es **Fischmuu**» (a made-up compound of *Muu*).
- I avoided «Hilf» because the l-vocalisation rule flags it and I'm unsure of «Hiuf», and used «Suech mit!» instead.
- I kept the existing «gygampfe» (verb) in dr-erscht-schueutag.

## Validation

A throwaway script checked:
- counts: 20 stories (6 × L1, 7 × L2, 7 × L3) and 10 songs;
- every text has 4 languages, and alignment groups are consistent across them;
- the structure rules for each level;
- choice `next` indices are valid and lead forward, all pages are reachable, and question answers are valid;
- all scene, option and question keys exist;
- the `words` ids exist;
- melody beats equal the sum of line beats;
- no forbidden BE forms appear (Grüezi, hoi, gönd, gaat, nöd, Meitli, chli, Wält, lachet, Grosätti, «En Guete», «mer» …).

All checks pass. The repo's `checkBernese` reports no new warnings beyond the existing onomatopoeia «wackel». `npx tsc -p tsconfig.app.json --noEmit` reports nothing for stories.ts or songs.ts.
